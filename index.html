TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & FAQ

Which broker and market data feed does GEXPIT use?

GEXPIT is engineered specifically for Interactive Brokers (IB Gateway). It directly captures the official OPRA SPX options tick stream through the IBKR API. You utilize the standard, low-cost market data subscription you already own with Interactive Brokers, with zero expensive third-party data subscriptions or vendor markups.

How does local data storage work and how do I build a personal historical database?

Every tick, trade execution, and computed Greeks surface is logged directly on your local machine in high-performance Columnar Apache Parquet format with Write-Ahead Log (WAL) architecture. Unlike cloud-locked web platforms, you hold 100% ownership of your raw and processed data. You build a valuable, private institutional tick archive day after day with zero recurring data archive fees.

What are the minimum system requirements to run the GEXPIT terminal smoothly?

Because GEXPIT renders real-time OLED heatmaps at 60 FPS and processes hundreds of thousands of ticks intraday via a Numba JIT-compiled engine, a standard modern PC is required. We recommend at least 8 GB of RAM (to comfortably run GEXPIT alongside Interactive Brokers TWS without bottlenecking memory) and a dedicated GPU with at least 4 GB of VRAM to guarantee zero-latency hardware-accelerated rendering.

How does the Replay Engine and the Intraday Live Rewind function?

GEXPIT features a dual-mode high-throughput replay engine: you can perform Full-Session Replays on any previously recorded day at variable speeds (1x to 300x) with zero data loss. Furthermore, during live market sessions, you can rewind the timeline to re-examine morning Initial Balance breakouts, heavy block sweeps, or key turning points, and then instantly return to live streaming with a single click (1-Click Live Snap).

How does the Flow Heatmap work and what are the display modes?

The heatmap renders Call and Put volume across the SPX strike chain in real time on a pure OLED black background, synchronized with the Rolling Volume Profile at the selected timeframe. It includes 10 display modes such as GEX Linear, GEX Logarithmic, Volume Flow, Smooth Flow, and Delta Dominance (weighted by absolute delta).

How do Multi-Timeframe Volume Profiles, Flow filters and Market Regime work?

Tab FLOWS HEATMAP lets you filter the Rolling Volume Profile and separate Call Flow & Put Flow across seven timeframes (1m to 120m). The HUD also tracks the Initial Balance (09:30–10:00 NY) with exact breakout metrics.

It also classifies the session into one of four Market Regimes derived from the relative position of Spot, Zero Gamma and Vol Trigger: Volatility Damping, Volatility Expansion, Delta Squeeze, and Volatility Cascade.

What is CVD, how does Lee-Ready classification work, and what are Sweep Alarms?

Each trade is classified with the Lee-Ready algorithm using a 250ms quote midpoint window. Trades above the midpoint are Buy, below are Sell, and at-midpoint trades are split 50/50 — preserving total volume without introducing artificial directional bias.

GEXPIT's Monetary CVD weights every execution by actual dollar notional rather than share count, isolating true directional pressure.

How are Greeks (GEX, VEX, DEX, CEX) and multi-strike exposures calculated in real time?

All five exposure panels are computed by a Numba JIT Black-Scholes engine and displayed as stacked Call/Put horizontal bars with three-level density color grading: LVN (low liquidity, grey), Neutral, and HVN (high density, full saturation).

How does GEX Dinamico differ from the standard Net GEX profile?

The standard Net GEX accumulates dealer exposure from historical tick flow. GEX Dinamico uses the total volume traded today per strike and recalculates the full gamma exposure using the current spot price and implied volatility via the Black-Scholes formula. When SPX moves, the entire profile is recalculated — walls shift, Zero Gamma migrates, and the Vol Trigger updates. The Vol Trigger is found dynamically with a price-space scan at hypothetical spot levels.
