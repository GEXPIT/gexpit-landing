01 / CALL & PUT FLOW

See which option strikes attract trading volume.

Compare Call and Put activity with the SPX price, on the same timeline.

Start with Combined Volume: bubbles show activity at each option strike (exercise price) over time. The price line shows where SPX is trading, also called spot. Switch views to separate Calls and Puts or compare which side has more volume.

Metrics, filters & display modes

Choose from 16 heatmap modes. Gamma views include GEX Linear, GEX Rolling, Wall Activity, GEX Velocity, GEX Absolute, Wall Absorption (GEX/Vol) and Charm Pressure (0DTE Pin). Volume views include Combined Volume, Volume Flow, Smooth Flow, Dominance, Delta Dominance, Duel, Call Only, Put Only and Dual Panels.

Measure activity by contract count, premium notional (option price × contracts × contract multiplier), or Delta weighting (which accounts for sensitivity to SPX price changes). Moneyness filters select strikes by their relationship to spot. Volume describes activity; Gamma views model exposure.

Read the screenshot: price, activity and the last 5 minutes

In the Combined Volume screenshot above, compare three visible elements:

  1. Centre: the white line traces SPX price; the rows of bubbles locate option activity at each strike over time.
  2. Left: Daily Vol Profile shows how volume is distributed across strikes over the session.
  3. Right: Rolling Profile is set to 5 min, showing the recent distribution. Compare its shape with the daily profile to see which strikes stand out in each window.

This comparison helps you separate the session total from recent activity. It does not establish why price moved or predict its next direction.

02 / ROLLING VOLUME & CVD

Compare the last few minutes with the full session.

A busy strike earlier in the day may be quieter now. Compare the daily and rolling profiles to see where recent volume concentrates.

Read Call and Put CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta) alongside price: each curve tracks the cumulative balance of estimated buying and selling activity. CVD Momentum shows how that balance is changing. The screenshot uses contract counts; Monetary CVD weights the flow by option premium notional.

Time windows & Monetary CVD

The Rolling Volume Profile offers windows from 1 minute to 120 minutes. Rolling volume peaks on spot can be compared over intervals from 1 minute to the full session.

Switch between contract volume, premium notional and Delta Flow, with separate Call/Put or net CVD views. Monetary CVD weights classified flow by option premium notional; classification is an estimate, not a record of participant intent.

Compare volume alerts & Net CVD

Watch the change, then inspect the context.

Enable volume alerts on the price chart to locate bursts of activity. Switch to Net CVD for a combined reading, then compare its cumulative curve with CVD Momentum to examine the recent change. The separate Call and Put view remains available for a closer look.

03 / DYNAMIC GEX

Compare Gamma exposure now with 5, 15 or 30 minutes ago.

See how the modeled exposure at each strike changes as SPX price and time evolve. Earlier markers appear when the session history is available.

Gamma measures how an option's sensitivity to price changes as SPX moves. GEX combines Gamma with activity to map exposure across strikes. Dynamic GEX recalculates that exposure using the current model Gamma; compare it with earlier readings or inspect Calls and Puts separately.

Calculation context & reference levels

Dynamic GEX revalues the full session volume at each strike with current model Gamma, using spot and the ATM Expected Move. It uses a flat ATM volatility assumption across strikes. Classic Net GEX instead accumulates flow contributions using Gamma at the time each contribution was recorded.

Net GEX and Dynamic GEX offer lookback markers at 1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes when that history is available. Split Total GEX does not use these markers. Dynamic GEX always uses the full session volume. Plotted references include Zero Gamma, Call Wall, Put Wall and Vol Trigger.

These are model-based references. They do not directly reveal dealer inventory or establish that a price level must hold.

02 VIEWS TO EXPLORE

Compare trading activity with the movement priced into options.

Implied volatility is derived from option prices. These views compare its changes, an estimated price range and the cost of an at the money (ATM) straddle: a Call and a Put at the same strike near spot.

Select a view. Click or tap the image to enlarge it.

Separate the range reference from the option price.

Expected Move provides a model-based range reference. The ATM straddle is an option price: Call plus Put. Compare their opening bands, follow the intraday straddle corridor, and check the observed straddle price against theoretical decay as expiry approaches.

Read volatility alongside volume and price.

A 30-minute Z-score expresses how far current ATM implied volatility (IV) is from its recent average, in standard deviation units. Compare the combined and separate Call/Put readings with volume per minute. Optional OI-VWAP, C-GAMMA, C-VOL and C-DELTA lines add options-derived references. OI-VWAP is synthetic: it is neither an ES futures VWAP nor a measurement of open interest.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Practical questions. Clear answers.

What PC, broker and market data do I need?

Platform: GEXPIT runs locally on a compatible Windows PC. The website works on mobile; the analytical application runs on your PC. Check hardware suitability during onboarding, especially if you run other trading applications alongside it.

Live analysis: the GEXPIT Extractor connects to Interactive Brokers through TWS or IB Gateway with the relevant market data permissions. It records local files that the GEXPIT cockpit reads. IBKR and market data fees are separate from GEXPIT access.

Replay: open compatible session files recorded by the Extractor, without an active broker connection. A historical data library is not included as a promise of early access.

What happens after I apply?

Your application is received for consideration. If selected, you will receive an invitation with activation instructions by email. Applying does not grant immediate access. The 21-day period starts when your license is activated.

What feedback is expected during the 21 days?

Use the platform and share concrete observations: setup difficulties, unclear controls or readings, reproducible bugs, or suggestions tied to your workflow. You do not need to find a bug or invent a feature to contribute. Honest, specific feedback is what matters.

What happens when the free period ends?

Free access ends after 21 days unless the developer grants an extension based on your contribution and ongoing testing needs. Without feedback, the free period is not renewed. Contributing does not guarantee an extension. Paid subscriptions will follow the invitation phase; pricing and terms will be provided before purchase.

Is GEXPIT for SPX options traders or ES futures traders?

Both can use SPX options activity as market context. GEXPIT analyzes SPX options; it is not an ES order-book feed. An options structure and a futures position have different exposures and risks, even when informed by the same market reading.

Do I need to know every indicator before starting?

No. Start with installation and a guided reading of one view. Instructions and explanatory videos help you understand the interface and how to examine 0DTE market activity. The tools still require learning; no indicator removes trading uncertainty.

Can I review past sessions?

Yes. Open compatible session files recorded by the GEXPIT Extractor for historical replay without an active broker connection. During live analysis, you can rewind the available session history and return to LIVE. An existing historical data library is not promised as part of early access.

How do I join Discord?

Discord is reserved for invited participants. Access information is shared with invitees; there is no public invite link on the website.

Does GEXPIT provide guaranteed trade signals?

No. GEXPIT is an analytical platform. Flow classification and exposure models have assumptions and limitations. A chart observation is not proof of a profitable trade, and the platform does not guarantee outcomes.
