TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & FAQ

Which broker and market data feed does GEXPIT use?

GEXPIT is engineered specifically for Interactive Brokers (TWS and IB Gateway). It directly captures the official OPRA SPXW options tick stream through the IBKR API. You utilize the standard, low-cost market data subscription you already own with Interactive Brokers, with zero expensive third-party data subscriptions or vendor markups.

How does local data storage work and how do I build a personal historical database?

Every tick, trade execution, and computed Greeks surface is logged directly on your local machine in high-performance Columnar Apache Parquet format with Write-Ahead Log (WAL) architecture. Unlike cloud-locked web platforms, you hold 100% ownership of your raw and processed data. You build a valuable, private institutional tick archive day after day with zero recurring data archive fees, ready for Python quantitative research (pandas/numpy/scipy) or automated strategies in TradeStation EasyLanguage.

How does the Replay Engine and the Intraday Live Rewind function?

GEXPIT features a dual-mode high-throughput replay engine: you can perform Full-Session Replays on any previously recorded day at variable speeds (1x to 50x) with zero data loss. Furthermore, during live market sessions, you can rewind the timeline to re-examine morning Initial Balance breakouts, heavy block sweeps, or key turning points, and then instantly return to live streaming with a single click (1-Click Live Snap) without missing newly arriving ticks.

How does the 3 Layer Gaussian Heatmap and its 5 normalization modes work?

Rather than displaying fragmented histograms, GEXPIT projects options liquidity onto a continuous 2D Gaussian density surface with statistical 70th-percentile isocurves and topological kernel calculations on uniform 5-point strike grids. It offers 5 distinct real-time processing modes:

  • Linear: Proportional absolute exposure across active strikes.
  • Logarithmic: Amplifies tail risk and far-OTM institutional sweeps.
  • Rolling (30-Minute): Highlights immediate intraday positioning shifts relative to recent activity.
  • Uniform 5pt Grid: Eliminates strike distortion across uniform 5-point grid calculations.
  • HVL Dual Flow: Dual-density separation isolating Call vs Put positioning with OLED background contrast fading.
How do Multi-Timeframe Volume Profiles and Call/Put Flow filters (1m to 120m) work?

In Tab 1, GEXPIT allows traders to dynamically filter both the Rolling Volume Profile and separate Call Flow & Put Flow across configurable timeframes: 1 min, 5 min, 10 min, 15 min, 30 min, 60 min, and 120 min (alongside the Full-Session Cumulative Daily Profile). It also computes real-time Market Regime classification and tracks the morning Initial Balance (09:30–10:00 NY) with exact breakout metrics and operational advisories.

What is Monetary CVD and how do Real-Time Sweep Alarms work?

Raw contract volume is misleading: 2,000 deep OTM contracts at $0.05 represent only $10,000 of capital, while 2,000 ATM contracts at $15.00 represent $3,000,000 and create massive hedging obligations for dealers. In Tab 2, GEXPIT's Monetary CVD weights every execution by actual dollar notional value (Contracts × Price × 100), tracks 1-minute Notional Value breakdown, and includes real-time Sweep Alarms with configurable dollar thresholds ($5M+) to immediately alert on aggressive Call and Put buying bursts.

How are Greeks (GEX, DEX, VEX, CEX) and Multi-Strike Exposures calculated in real time?

In Tab 3, GEXPIT executes a Numba JIT C-compiled Black-Scholes solver to dynamically compute a 4-panel synchronized exposure matrix by strike: Total GEX (Gamma Dollar Hedging $/pt), Total VEX (Vanna cross-sensitivity ∂Δ/∂σ), Total DEX (Net Dollar Delta), and Total CEX (Charm time-decay of delta). Each panel features stacked Call/Put horizontal bars, 3-state density color grading (Low Void, Neutral, High Density HVL), and an overlaid synchronized Spot curve.

How does the GEXPIT Net GEX Profile and its Historical Shift Markers (1m, 5m, 15m, 30m) work?

In Tab 4 (GEXPIT), the screen is split into a Time-Series Spot chart (featuring dynamic continuous Zero Gamma, Max Call Wall, and Max Put Wall) and a Net GEX Profile by strike. The Net GEX chart displays historical scatter markers at 1m (white), 5m (cyan), 15m (yellow), and 30m (orange) ago, allowing traders to visually observe how dealer walls are migrating, strengthening, or decaying in real time.

How does Big Trades detection and Strike Proximity filtering (≤15pt) function?

In Tab 5 (Big Trades), GEXPIT maps large institutional block executions directly onto the Spot price trajectory as color-coded square blocks (Green for Call, Red for Put). Traders can filter trades by Strike Proximity (≤15pt from Spot) to isolate high-urgency ATM sweeps that force immediate ES futures hedging, and set custom Dollar Notional Thresholds ($500k+) to eliminate small retail noise.

What are the platform's graphical and rendering optimizations?
